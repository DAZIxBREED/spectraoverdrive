# SpectraOverdrive 0.4 Fixture Profiles

## Moving Head 13-channel

0 dimmer, 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 pan coarse, 5 pan fine, 6 tilt coarse, 7 tilt fine, 8 strobe, 9 zoom, 10 gobo, 11 gobo rotation, 12 prism.

## PAR 5-channel

0 dimmer, 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 strobe.

## Blinder 2-channel

0 dimmer, 1 strobe.

## Strobe 3-channel

0 dimmer, 1 strobe speed, 2 effect/mode.

## Light Bar 5-channel

0 dimmer, 1 red, 2 green, 3 blue, 4 strobe.

The factories create these as starting points. Their exact channel maps can be edited with `SpectraFixtureChannelMap`.
